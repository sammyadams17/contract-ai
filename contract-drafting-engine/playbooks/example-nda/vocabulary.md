# Vocabulary (example client)

What the client's words mean, for the reader of a term sheet. It tells the reader what kinds of
term to look for, never what a term sheet contains.

- **Purpose**: also "Permitted Purpose", "the Project", "the Proposed Transaction".
- **Confidential Information**: also "Evaluation Material", "Proprietary Information".
- **Term**: also "Duration", "Confidentiality Period"; the period the obligations last, which a
  term sheet may give separately from how long the agreement itself runs.
- **Permitted Recipients**: also "Representatives", "Authorised Persons".
- **Return or Destruction**: also "Return of Materials", "Retention".
- **NDA**, **CDA**: non-disclosure agreement, confidentiality agreement.
