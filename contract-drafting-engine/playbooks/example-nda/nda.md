# Mutual NDA playbook (example client)

An example of a client playbook built on the general one. Each section after `## General (NDA)`
is named after a clause or schedule of the client's template, and its rules reach only the piece
the planner gives that section. "Read also by:" names other sections whose pieces must read this
one too.

## General (NDA)

- The agreement is mutual unless the term sheet says one party discloses only. Where it is
  one-way, "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" replace "each party" wherever the obligation
  binds only the recipient, and the obligations that would bind the discloser are struck.

## Cover page

- The parties are named as the term sheet names them, each with its company number and
  registered office where the term sheet gives them; a missing number is `[•]` with one open
  point.

## Definitions

- "Purpose" is the term sheet's purpose, in its words, never widened.
- "Confidential Information" keeps the template's list; a category the term sheet adds is one
  more limb, in the template's style.

## Confidentiality Obligations

- The standard of care is the template's unless the term sheet sets another.
- Disclosure to advisers and affiliates follows the template; a further class of permitted
  recipient the term sheet allows is one more limb.

## Exceptions

- The template's exceptions stand. An exception the term sheet adds is one more limb; an
  exception it removes is struck with an open point, since a narrower set of exceptions is a
  departure from the house position.

## Term and Termination

- The term is the term sheet's. The confidentiality obligations survive for the period the term
  sheet gives; where it gives none, the template's survival period stands.

Read also by: Return or Destruction

## Return or Destruction

- The template's clause stands unless the term sheet sets a period or allows retention for
  legal or regulatory purposes, which is written as one more sentence.

## Remedies

- The template's injunctive relief clause stands. A liability cap or indemnity the term sheet
  asks for is written here, with one open point, since the template has none.

## Governing Law and Jurisdiction

- As the General rule: the term sheet's where it states them.

## Execution page

- The signatories are the parties as defined. A signatory's name or title the term sheet does
  not give is left as the template's blank.
